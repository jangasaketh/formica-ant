    1 of 2 · What this is

    You are one ant, going down after your queen

    The situation

    A black colony raided your nest and carried your queen five floors down into their burrow. Everyone else is dead. You are going after her.

    What you do on each floor

    Eat until your crop is full, kill the champion guarding the shaft, then drop down it. Five floors, then she is yours.

    Why food matters

    It is not points. The shaft stays shut until your crop is full — an ant with nothing in her does not make the next floor.

    When you are lost

    The bar at the top always names your next job and points at it. Hold Q and a scent trail shows you the way.
